# Related Work

### OpenDrift v1.0: a generic framework for trajectory modelling:
https://gmd.copernicus.org/articles/11/1405/2018/

Building upon physical parameters, frameworks like OpenDrift offer an open-source, modular, Python-based Lagrangian architecture to simulate particle trajectories.   

By dynamically ingesting multi-source Eulerian forcing fields (ocean currents, winds, and waves), the framework enables efficient, large-scale stochastic drift simulations for emergency preparedness and operational planning.   


### Research on drift trajectories of typical marine search and rescue objects based on wind-wave-current coupled experiments
https://www.sciencedirect.com/science/article/pii/S0141118725004134

Physical and experimental investigations—such as wind-wave-current coupled studies—provide critical baseline data on how real-world search and rescue (SAR) targets (e.g., life rafts and fishing vessels) respond to complex environmental forces.   

These studies capture fine-scale hydrodynamic interactions, wave-induced drift, and leeway coefficients that are necessary to accurately parameterize target movement.  


### Linking Drift Prediction to Search Planning in Maritime Search and Rescue: A Review of Cross-Stage Evaluation
https://www.mdpi.com/2077-1312/14/20/1886

While prediction models generate particle clouds or probability distributions, recent reviews emphasize the critical need to bridge the gap between drift prediction and search planning.
These syntheses evaluate how uncertainty outputs are translated into actionable search metrics—such as the probability of containment and resource allocation—while assessing the practical utility and limitations of emerging AI-driven optimization methods.
